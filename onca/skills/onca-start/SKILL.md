# Start with Onca

If Onca's tools are missing or a call says to sign in, use the onca-sign-in skill first.

The founder's own words for this step: "Then call Onca's start_launch and follow it: do the work, ask me one simple question at a time, and get my first post ready for my approval."

So call Onca's `start_launch` (the project has no channel or no post yet; otherwise call `get_launch_overview`) and follow its steps. You do the work and the founder only answers and approves.
